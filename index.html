Install / update ForkStride

Open this page in Safari on your registered iPhone. This is an Ad Hoc test build, not TestFlight and not a public App Store release.

Install on your iPhone

  1. Tap Install ForkStride and confirm the installation prompt.
  2. Return to the Home Screen and wait for the download to finish.
  3. Open ForkStride. Choose which Apple Health permissions you want to grant.

Registered devices only. Only devices included in the signing profile can install this build. If installation fails, send the exact message or a screenshot. Do not delete an existing app with saved data just to retry.

Watch installation and HealthKit behavior still need physical-device testing. A successful cloud build does not prove those integrations work on your devices.

New in build 4

Safer recovery; diary, weight and workout corrections with deletion/undo; tracking-aware history and Watch entry; 5,794 offline reference foods; 174 generic exercises; improved portions, search, timers and planned sets.

Export a backup first, then update over your existing app to preserve your records. Your registered iPhone and Watch remain supported. This is a test build; camera, Health and paired-Watch behavior still need testing on your devices.

Your registered Watch

Update ForkStride on your paired iPhone first. If it does not appear automatically on the Watch, open the iPhone Watch app → My Watch → Apps / Available Apps, find ForkStride and tap Install. Menu wording varies by OS version. Apple's installation instructions.

At the gym, saved foods and workouts work offline. New product lookups require internet, and Watch synchronization waits for a connection. The installer does not require your home Wi-Fi.
